# Response To Reviewers Outline: Table Consolidation And Checklist Tone

## Reviewer Concern

The paper risked reading like a collection of checklists and tables rather than a unified methodological argument.

## Revision Response

We revised the main text to make the role of the tables more explicit and to reduce checklist-like presentation. In particular, Section VI now frames reporting and reproducibility as claim-evidence calibration across three evidence tiers, rather than as a flat list of required parameters.

## Key Changes

- Table 8 was renamed `Evidence Tiers for Retrieval-Based Audit Research`.
- Implementation transparency is now organized around three artifacts: source inventory, retrieval recipe, and output linkage file.
- Sensitivity guidance is framed as proportional to the inference being made.
- The manuscript appendix summary was shortened so the main text does not list every appendix table.
- Detailed field-level reporting items remain in the online supplement and replication package.

## Planned Response-Letter Language

Suggested response:

> We revised the table presentation and surrounding prose to make the manuscript read as a methodological argument rather than as a checklist. Section VI now explains that reporting requirements scale with claim strength: a Tier 1 methodological demonstration requires inspectable retrieval artifacts and claim-level linkage, while Tier 2 and Tier 3 studies require stronger sensitivity, expert coding, benchmark, and robustness evidence. We also shortened the appendix summary in the manuscript and retained detailed field-level reporting items in the online supplement, where they serve reproducibility rather than interrupting the main argument.

## Residual Boundary

The paper remains intentionally detailed because implementation transparency is central to the contribution. The revision reduces checklist tone in the main text without weakening the reproducibility package.
